## My position and tasks

Since September 2026, I have been a **work-study ERP consultant**. The first weeks are dedicated to **training on the Open-Prod ERP**, on **demo databases**, before working with customers: **purchase-to-sale flows**, **project-based management**, **production flows**, and **administration of my test environment** (ERP updates, duplicating, restoring and importing databases).

On 16 October 2026, I presented a **complete flow in Open-Prod** to the whole consulting team and to management (technical, project and regional directors), from the sale of a manufactured product to its invoice, with one step outsourced to a **subcontractor**: this is the project page "Production flow with subcontracting".

In October 2026, I am also taking a **two-day training course on QWeb**, the Odoo templating engine that Open-Prod is built on, which lays out the ERP's **reports**: **creating a report**, **editing an existing report template**, and searching the **SQL tables** for the data to display.
