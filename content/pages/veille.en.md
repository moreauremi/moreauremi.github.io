I follow three topics, linked to my work-study placement and my projects: the **security of industrial companies**, the **virtualisation** that runs their servers, and the **e-invoicing** reform, which is changing their business software.

## Method

**Every Monday**, a **tool I built** for this portfolio reads about **thirty specialised sources** (including **CERT-FR**, the French national cybersecurity agency's response team), then an **AI** (GitHub Copilot) picks the most useful articles for each topic, summarises them and tags them. I receive this news **by e-mail**, **read the selected articles**, then **write my syntheses**: what I take away, what it changes for companies, what I do with it. I write them in my dashboard, or directly by replying to the e-mail: the AI then formats my opinion without adding anything, and I **proofread the synthesis before publishing it**.

The news and my syntheses are written in French, as are the sources, which are mostly French: the topics concern French companies.
