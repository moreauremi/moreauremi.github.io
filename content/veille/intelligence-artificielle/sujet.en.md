## Why this topic

**Artificial intelligence** is turning up everywhere in companies: **assistants** in office tools, **agents** able to act on their own, AI built into **ERPs** and business software. It saves time, but it also raises real questions: **data leaks** when information is handed to an online tool, **mistakes** stated with confidence, **attacks** made easier, and a legal framework taking shape with the European **AI Act** and the recommendations of the **CNIL** (the French data protection authority).

The topic concerns me directly: my own technology watch is sorted and summarised by an **AI** (GitHub Copilot), and my cybersecurity summaries often come back to the role of AI in attacks. This watch helps me know what an SME can **use safely**, and what it needs to **keep under control**.
