## Why this topic

Almost all SME servers run on **virtual machines**. Since **Broadcom's takeover of VMware**, the **end of perpetual licences** and **rising prices** have pushed many companies to **switch hypervisors**, towards **Hyper-V**, **Proxmox VE** or **XCP-ng**.

The topic concerns me directly: at work, we use **Hyper-V** to create our virtual machines locally, and my homelab runs on **Proxmox**. This watch helps me compare the two worlds, and know what I would advise an SME that has to choose.
