## Why this topic

**E-invoicing** is becoming **mandatory** for all French companies. Since **September 2026**, they must all be able to **receive** electronic invoices; SMEs and micro-businesses will also have to **issue** them from **September 2027**, through an **approved platform**.

I am right in the middle of it: at the ERP integrator where I work, we partner with **IOPOLE**, an approved platform, to help our customers through the reform. This watch lets me follow the timetable, the formats (**Factur-X**, **UBL**, **CII**) and the questions companies are asking.
